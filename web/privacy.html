Privacy Policy

Last updated July 7, 2026

Life Journal is built to keep your journaling private. This policy explains what we handle and what we don’t.

What stays on your device

Your journals, daily pages, plans, handwriting, Bible-study notes, and settings are stored locally on your device (in the app’s private storage). We do not have access to this content and it is never uploaded unless you explicitly choose to.

Optional cloud sync

If you turn on cloud sync, your journal data is encrypted in transit and stored under a sync code (or your Apple sign-in) so you can share it across your own devices. Anyone with your sync code can read that data, so keep it private. You can stop syncing at any time.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive only a stable anonymous identifier used to associate your synced data. We do not receive your name or email unless you provide it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Generated Bible studies and plans

When you request a generated Bible study or a twelve-week plan, the passage reference or the goal text you enter is sent to our server, which uses a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the result. This text is used only to generate your response and is not used to build an advertising profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in these prompts.

Calendar

If you grant calendar access, Life Journal reads your events only to display them alongside your daily schedule, on your device. It is read-only — we never modify your calendar and calendar data is not sent to our servers.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They do not require or use a remote push service.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your data.
  • We don’t show third-party ads.
  • We don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Life Journal is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
